Dot-matrix reference and application adaptation
=============================================

Reference: https://dotmatrix.zzzzshawn.cloud/
Source: https://github.com/zzzzshawn/matrix
Reviewed 17 November 2026.

The local matrix-text.mjs renderer is an application-specific text adapter
inspired by the source's explicit dot-grid layout and currentColor dots. Its
5x7 glyph map and SVG text rendering are local additions. This prototype does
not bundle the source's React components or present them as a standalone
reusable component library.
