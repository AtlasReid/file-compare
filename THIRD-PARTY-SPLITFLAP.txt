split-flap.mjs and split-flap.css adapt the cached top/bottom/falling cell,
bottom-center hinge, split overlay, card material, forward character spool and
the original 25ms-step / 360ms-final-fall motion. At the user's request, the
local adapter extends that reference into a two-sided 180-degree leaf for EVERY
character, including intermediates. Its 80:160 timing weights and landed hold
are application choices, normalized into explicit playback frames and native
WAAPI changes rather than the source's flat intermediate swaps.
It does not include the source application's server, remote pairing, networking,
audio, external fonts, message scheduler or weather features.
